Privacy Policy

This page explains how VentrixCarros may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the platform.

This is a frontend project policy template. Before launching a real service, have the final policy reviewed for the jurisdictions and data-processing activities that apply to your business.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you create an account, complete your profile, search for jobs, save opportunities, submit applications, or contact support.

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number, and location.
  • Professional experience, skills, education, and career goals.
  • Resume content, portfolio links, and professional profiles.
  • Job searches, saved jobs, applications, and preferences.
  • Messages and feedback sent to our support team.

Information collected automatically

Depending on how the platform is implemented, we may collect device, browser, usage, log, and basic analytics information to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to provide and improve the VentrixCarros experience, including:

  • Creating and managing your account.
  • Showing job recommendations based on your preferences.
  • Providing resume, interview, and career tools.
  • Tracking saved jobs and application activity.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Detecting misuse, protecting accounts, and improving security.
  • Understanding platform performance and feature usage.

3. AI tools and generated content

VentrixCarros may offer AI-assisted resume analysis, job matching, interview preparation, cover-letter generation, career roadmaps, and salary insights.

AI outputs are suggestions and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for a particular situation. You should review generated content before using it in an application or professional decision.

Salary insights are estimates and are not guarantees of compensation, hiring outcomes, or employment.

4. Information sharing

We do not treat your personal information as public by default. Information may be shared when necessary to operate the platform, provide requested services, comply with law, prevent harm, or protect the rights and security of users and the service.

If you choose to make your profile visible to employers, the information included in that profile may be viewed by relevant employers or platform users according to your settings.

5. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so you should use a strong password and avoid sharing account credentials.

Passwords should be securely hashed and authentication should be handled by a protected backend in a production implementation.

6. Your choices

Depending on the applicable law and platform implementation, you may be able to:

  • Access or update information in your profile.
  • Change notification and visibility preferences.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Contact us with questions about data handling.

Some information may need to be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, contact us at:

privacy@ventrixcarros.com
